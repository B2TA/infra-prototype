# Local project guidance

- This is a disposable proof-of-concept deployment.
- Prefer the simplest clear solution over production-grade best practices.
- Avoid speculative abstractions, hardening, high availability, and extra infrastructure unless explicitly requested.
- Keep changes small and easy to remove.
- Preserve basic credential safety: do not commit or expose secrets.
- Call out important limitations, but do not expand the scope to solve them automatically.
